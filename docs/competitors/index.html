  • PN

    Priya Nair

    Fastest growing

    @priyadaily · Day in the Life · Career

    +24.5%growth9.6% engagement

    Why it’s working: Priya turned an oversaturated format into a retention machine by narrating ordinary days like short stories — a beginning, a snag, a payoff.

    Repeated patterns — expand for the evidence

    Adaptable to your voice

    • Open on the complication, not a greeting
    • Alternate how-tos with personal stories
    • Narrate with specifics — times, numbers, names
    • Let quiet moments breathe instead of cutting them

    Specific to them — don’t chase these

    • Three years of daily-vlog back catalog feeding recommendations
    • A “day with Priya” ritual her audience already expects
    • A partner who films B-roll every day
    Cadence
    Three fixed slots a week — Mon / Wed / Fri
    Hook style
    Conflict first — the day’s snag in one line
    CTA style
    Soft ask — “come back Friday to see how it went”

    Latest: "Day in the life" with voiceover storytelling

    What you can try

    • Test one day-in-the-life montage narrated in your own voiceView opportunity
    • Open your next educational post on the snag — story before adviceSee your related observation
  • AT

    Ava Torres

    @avatorres · Dating · Lifestyle

    +18.2%growth8.9% engagement

    Why it’s working: Ava manufactures curiosity — every video is built around one question viewers can’t answer themselves, and the payoff lands only at the end.

    Repeated patterns — expand for the evidence

    Adaptable to your voice

    • Lead with a question the viewer can’t answer alone
    • Change something on screen every ten seconds
    • Hold the best moment for the final third

    Specific to them — don’t chase these

    • The street-interview format itself — it needs strangers, a city, and a camera operator
    • A recognizable on-camera interviewer persona built over two years
    • Reshare loops from interviewees posting their own clips
    Cadence
    Theme weeks — daily for five days, then a pause
    Hook style
    A twist question in the first breath
    CTA style
    Comment bait — “whose answer was right?”

    Latest: Street interviews with a twist question

    What you can try

    • Apply her twist-question open to your founder-dating angleView opportunity
    • Test a debate-style comment CTA on your next dating postGenerate brief
  • JL

    Jordan Lee

    @jordanmakes · Career · Finance

    +11.7%growth7.2% engagement

    Why it’s working: Jordan wins on receipts — every claim is backed by a number on screen, which makes the advice feel verifiable instead of aspirational.

    Repeated patterns — expand for the evidence

    Adaptable to your voice

    • Put the real number on screen early
    • Show the raw screen instead of a cleaned-up graphic
    • End on one copyable action, not a summary

    Specific to them — don’t chase these

    • A decade of finance-industry credibility behind the claims
    • Anonymized salary data sourced from his newsletter audience
    • An audience already trained to expect receipts every week
    Cadence
    Weekly, same slot — Sunday evenings, plus a monthly interview
    Hook style
    The number first, the story second
    CTA style
    One copyable script or template per video

    Latest: Salary breakdown with on-screen receipts

    What you can try

    • Put a real number on screen in the first ten seconds of a salary postView opportunity
    • Test a receipts-style money diary in your own voiceGenerate brief
