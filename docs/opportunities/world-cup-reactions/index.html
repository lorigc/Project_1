Practical Strategy

Formatting & narrative blueprints

Same-day turnaround

Each moment’s window is roughly a day. Film within hours or skip the moment entirely.

Niche lens

React as who you are — the builder, not a pundit. Borrowed authority reads instantly false.

Single-take reaction

One take, real surprise, context added as on-frame captions afterward.

Caption the context

Assume the casual viewer knows nothing — one caption card carries them through.
